About Asteroid Shooter

Asteroid Shooter is a browser arcade game on Base. Players connect a wallet and play.

The AST token

AST is the in-game reward token, minted to players based on leaderboard results.

Contract on Base: 0xcEFB2484932595ffFC809a27B8A5b106A8E1715A

AST was never sold: there was no ICO or presale. It is not an investment.
